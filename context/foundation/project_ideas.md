I would like to create an app for families, especially with children who have to manage the list of pharmaceuticals they have at home. Some pharmaceuticals are used quite rarely so you might not remember you have them and simply but another packet. Also there are many pharmaceuticals that work in the same way (zamienniki) and different doctors might recommend different brands so you end up with 3 boxes with different names that do the same. So the basic functionalities would be adding a new pharmaceutical, extracting its main active substance and saving it. Additional steps would be including the expiration date (and alerts based on that) and extracting the name from the bar code or photo of the package with name. It has to be accessible from phone but first no native app, just web. It should allow sharing the information with other household members. Other functionalities might include storing additional information on dosage, how many times per day, with food or not, etc. There can also be reminders on when to take a pharmaceutical - it can be for any member of the household. Children in the household would not be able to manage it but can be assigned a drug to take. We can also incllude the doctors recommendations to set up the drug routine (when to take what and dosage), automtically compare it with drugs we have and recommend what we should buy. Based on the drug routine and checks whether we took sth or not it can also calculate whether we still have enough pills
